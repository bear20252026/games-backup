the sketchbook

small webgl experiments, each on its own page. a few of them grew into the bigger projects in this meadow.

sketchbook.curllmooha.com↗

  1. 01 recursive rounded grid — A compact grid of soft-edged blocks continuously divides and reorganises itself in three dimensions.
  2. 02 animated subdivision pillars — A warm architectural field divides one footprint into rounded pillars of changing size and height.
  3. 03 reactive blade ring — A circular array of soft capsule blades bends and changes colour around an orbiting light.
  4. 04 raymarched liquid shells — Three glossy spherical shells dissolve into noisy openings around a luminous inner core.
  5. 05 noise-driven voxel cube — Up to one thousand rounded voxels form a cube that erodes, rotates, and rebuilds through a moving noise field.
  6. 06 procedural lego terrain — Thousands of studded toy bricks assemble into a stepped terrain of waves, ridges, and craters.
  7. 07 rolling ball puck field — A plain white ball sinks into a dense field of rounded pucks, pressing a smooth bowl as it rolls and leaving a wake that glows coral, then fades through magenta and indigo to white.
  8. 08 proximity fracture sphere — A pre-fractured dark sphere opens toward the pointer and reveals an electric blue interior.
  9. 09 kinetic coin ring — Fifteen metallic coin-like discs rotate in a circular arrangement under warm reflected light.
  10. 10 iridescent water drop — A drop of water turning slowly, refracting the room behind it and breaking into spectrum where its surface turns away from you.
  11. 11 physics glass typography — The word “TOUCH” becomes a set of refractive rigid bodies that can be pushed around the studio.
  12. 12 kinetic tile grid — An eight-by-eight surface opens selected tiles from hinged edges, automatically or under your pointer.
  13. 13 twisted cylinder ribbon — A responsive line of coloured cylinders twists into a continuous ribbon-like wave.
  14. 14 layered disc repulsion — Five dense layers of small discs repel, tilt, and brighten around an oscillating luminous sphere.
  15. 15 chromatic domino trail — Touch any piece in a winding installation of colourful, pip-marked dominoes to release a two-way physical chain reaction.
  16. 16 sunlit shore — A slice of coast held in a glass tank: palms on the dune, a school working the shallows, a whale in the deep end, and a sea that turns bioluminescent after dark.
  17. 17 shoal — Looking up through open water at a school of jacks circling beneath the sun, then travelling, drifting apart and closing ranks again while light shafts fall through the swell.
  18. 18 two shores — Brush away a beach to find the open sea underneath, where a whole day passes from dawn through sunset to a starlit night - or freeze it all into Antarctica.
