questions clients ask

before you write

do you only build 3d?

no. plenty of my work is creative websites with no 3d at all, where gsap does the motion. i’ll suggest 3d only where it earns its place.

do you work with clients outside india?

yes. i’m in india (ist) and work remotely. most of the talking happens over email, with a call when it helps.

how much does a 3d website cost?

it depends on how much is in it. a single 3d scene on a site you already have costs a lot less than a full 3d site built from scratch. tell me your budget and i’ll say honestly what fits in it.

how long does a 3d website take?

it depends on how much is in it. after the first conversation i’ll send you a timeline and a price before any work starts.

will it run on phones?

yes, that’s part of the job, not an extra. this whole portfolio is a 3d meadow and it runs on a mid-range android phone.

can you work inside my react or next.js app?

yes. i build with react three fiber, which sits inside a react or next.js app like any other component. plain three.js works anywhere else.

i only have a design in figma. is that enough?

yes. polify started as a static figma file and i took it to the live site on my own.

what do you need from me to start?

what the site is for, any brand files or 3d models you have, a few references you like, and a rough budget.

got something in mind?

a few lines on what it’s for and when you need it is plenty to start.
